# Helion Architecture: Rain on Glass

Part of the [Helion architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md). The glass composite itself is in [water-optics.md](water-optics.md), Glass; this file holds how rain on glass grew, newest pass last.

Glass, third pass (2026-10-29, owner request: rain on glass like a photo of a wet window): drops come in three bead layers (7, 16 and 40 cells per block, 40 %, 70 % and 85 % of the cells, radius 0.14 to 0.32 of a cell, outline wobbled by 12 % in three lobes) and drops smaller than 1.2 pixels fade out instead of turning into specks. A drop bends the view by 3.5 times its size, so it shows a small upside down image of the world, darkens to 60 % at its rim, brightens its lower rim with focused light and catches a highlight of the reflection. Upright panes have 6 run columns per block (75 % used) whose wavy path wanders by a sine and noise; the trail behind a head is 2.5 blocks long, swells and thins with noise, bends the view across it and has a dark rim and a glint line. Between drops the pane mists over: 50 % of a 10 tap blur of the view behind (radius 0.035 over the distance, at most 3 % of the screen), 15 % grayed and 12 % lighter; drops and trails clear the mist. Visual test: `glass_window_rain`.

Glass, fourth pass (2026-10-29, owner feedback in game: wall windows showed no drops, drops too sparse, runs too fast and cut off): rain on glass ramps in from sky light 0.5 to 0.8 instead of a hard 0.85 cut, so windows in walls under an overhang (whose nearest face lights from inside) get drops too; bead layers are 10, 24 and 56 cells per block (35 %, 75 %, 90 %), rims darken to 70 %, glints only appear on drops of 3 to 8 pixels and more (small ones were white specks); runs slide at 0.06 to 0.2 blocks per second with a 6 block period and a 3 block trail whose last 30 % fades out, so the trail never ends in a cut; mist 60 %.

Glass, fifth pass (2026-10-29, owner feedback in game: wall window drops looked like brown dirt): the highlight of a drop took the reflection, which indoors is the dark wooden room; it now takes the brighter of the reflection and 1.3 times the brightness of the view through the pane, glints show from 5 pixels, and rims darken to 35 % instead of 70 %.

Glass, sixth pass (2026-10-29, owner feedback in game: drops on glass over water looked like flickering specks, the pane looked foggy, glass underground got drops): the mist is gone and so is the smallest bead layer (56 per block, below 3 pixels it shimmered); beads are 8 and 18 cells per block (45 % and 70 %) and trails swell by 30 % instead of 90 %, so they read as continuous lines. Rain no longer follows the sky light of the face (a cave 7 blocks from an opening still had level 8): `WaterRainCover` holds the vanilla rain floor (`MOTION_BLOCKING` height) of 64 x 64 columns around the camera and a glass face within 28 blocks is wet when rain reaches the cell above a horizontal face, or the cell on either side of an upright face, exactly where vanilla rain falls. With water behind the glass, the drops bend the water pixel too (the water is shaded again at the bent pixel when it is still water), so drops over water show a lens view instead of only their rim. Visual test: `glass_lake_rain`, `stained_light_rain`.

Glass, seventh pass (2026-10-29, owner feedback in game: drops on a pane seen at a glancing angle were dark flickering specks, worst along its edge): the drop footprint came from `fwidth` of the plane coordinates, which is undefined in 2 x 2 quads that lose pixels to the early `discard` at glass edges and tiny along one axis at glancing angles. `helionGlassFootprint` now intersects the rays of the neighbor pixels with the glass plane and takes the larger step, so drops fade by their real size on screen (from 1.5 to 4 pixels, glints from 6). Visual test: `glass_window_rain_above` looks down along the panes from above.

Glass, eighth pass (2026-10-29, owner feedback in game: drops on upright glass seen up close looked like large speckled half discs, and runs zigzagged): the lens shift of a drop is capped at its own size on screen (bend times 2.5 over the larger footprint step), because close to the eye or at a glancing angle the projected shift outgrew the drop and sampled scattered pixels; runs lose the sine wiggle and the meander and fall straight down, each keeping one random offset in its column. Visual tests: `glass_block_rain_close`, `glass_lake_rain_low`.

Glass, ninth pass (2026-10-29, owner feedback in game: drops still shimmered; the owner asked for a simple wet window, drops, mist and running water, not optical realism): a drop shifted the view by 2.5 times (trails 5 times) its offset from its center, which shows a small upside down image minified 1.5 to 4 times; the sampled texels jumped with every jitter of the temporal anti-aliasing and read as shimmer. Drops now shift by 0.45 of their offset (trails the same), which magnifies the view behind them 1.8 times without minifying anything, so the view inside a drop is as stable as the view through the pane, and the cap on the shift is gone. The pane mists over again: where no drop or trail lies, 55 % of the rain amount mixes in a 16 tap golden angle blur of the view behind (radius 1.2 % of the screen height, only when the pane is alone in front of terrain; in front of water or the sky only the veil), grayed by 25 %, lifted 8 % and veiled by 0.05. Visual tests: `glass_block_rain_close`, `covered_rain`.

Glass, tenth pass: rebuilt (2026-10-29, owner feedback in game: drops on wall windows shimmered and were faint, the mist was barely visible, the drop look was disliked; a glass roof seen from below looked fine). The drops were analytic per pixel and faded out below 3 to 6 pixels, so on windows, seen farther and more obliquely than a roof overhead, most drops sat on that threshold and blinked under temporal anti-aliasing (L-189). Rain on glass now reads two mipmapped textures from `GlassRainMaps` with `textureGrad`, the gradients from the neighbor pixel rays intersected with the glass plane (`helionGlassPlaneSteps`), so drops too small to resolve average into a wet tone instead of vanishing:
1. Beads: the bead map twice, at 0.5 and, mirrored and shifted, 1 tile per block; a drop shows when its seed is below 80 % (fine layer 55 %) of the rain amount, grows in over 12 % of a 75 s life and fades in the last 2 %, as a threshold on its cone profile with an edge as soft as the pixel footprint; from mip level 1 to 3 the cover blends into the averaged profile.
2. Runs, on upright panes: the streak map at 1 x 0.5 tiles per block; each path (seed from its texel and the block column) runs when its seed is below 70 % of the rain, at 0.12 to 0.4 blocks per second with a stick and slip wobble, every 8 blocks of travel; a round head 0.035 blocks long uses the full path width, the 2.5 block trail 45 % of it, thinning out after 35 %; the head wipes the beads off the whole path width, and they come back over the rest of the cycle.
3. Shading: a drop shifts the view behind it toward its center by 0.45 of its lens offset (magnifying), darkens by up to 50 % toward its rim, brightens its lower rim by the view through the pane, gathers 80 % of the view 0.3 blocks across from its center into its rim (an inverted lens image, so drops near a torch glow at night), reflects the sky with water Fresnel times 2.5 on a normal tilted by its rim and catches sun and moon glints.
4. Mist: 70 % of the rain amount, patchy (55 to 100 % from a 0.7 per block noise) and grained by the bead map at 3 tiles per block; a 16 tap golden angle blur over 2 % of the screen height, rotated per pixel, then 45 % toward a haze of its gray and the sky ambient, lifted 6 % and veiled by 0.06. Drops, trails and wiped run paths are clear.
Visual tests: `glass_window_rain`, `glass_block_rain_close`, `glass_pane_room_rain`, `glass_window_rain_above`, `covered_rain`, `glass_lake_rain`.

Glass, eleventh pass (2026-10-30, owner feedback in game: drops on thin panes seen at a glancing angle crawled and looked grainy): in the averaged far range the lens direction was the normalized average of the mip level, a near zero vector whose direction jumped from pixel to pixel, so the tilted drop normal, its sky reflection, glints, crescent and gathered light turned into per pixel noise; the sky term also reached 2.5 times the sky at a grazing view. The lens direction is now divided by at least 0.15 so a faint average stays faint, every directional term is scaled by the drop detail (1 minus the far blend, which now runs from mip level 0.5 to 2), glints roughen to 0.35 with the far blend, and the drop reflects only the Fresnel it adds over the flat pane.

Glass, twelfth pass (2026-10-30, owner screenshot in game: drops on roofs looked right, on upright faces they were horizontal speckled streaks that crawled): the glass normal comes from screen derivatives of the position in `water/surface.fsh` and carries per pixel noise; horizontal faces use fixed plane axes, but upright faces took their horizontal axis from that normal, and with world coordinates of up to 1024 blocks a 0.001 error moved the plane coordinate by about a block from pixel to pixel (L-190). `helionGlassNormal` now snaps a glass normal whose largest component exceeds 0.9 to that axis, and `helionOnGlassFace` moves the rain position onto the face plane rounded to 1/16 of a block along the view ray, so the depth buffer error at glancing angles no longer moves it either.
