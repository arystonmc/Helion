### `com.aryston.helion.render.water`

#### WaterSurfaceStage
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterSurfaceStage.java`
- Role: Pass `water_surface` after the solid features, only when ray traced water reflections, screen space reflections or realistic water are on and the terrain supports it (binds the glossy mask for the glass pipelines): draws the translucent terrain layer again into `helion:water_surface` (RGBA16_FLOAT color cleared to zero plus its own depth), with the opaque scene depth as a sampler, so it holds the nearest translucent surface in front of the opaque scene. The layer is drawn twice (`render` and `renderGlass`, the second with its own uniform ring and `WaterRain.w` set): the water pass keeps only water sprites and writes the octahedral normal (animated waves on flat surfaces), the thickness to the opaque surface behind and the sky light; the glass pass keeps every other translucent block (ice, glass, stained glass, panes) and writes its face normal, the glossy marker `-1 - thickness` (`helionEncodeGlossy`) and the sky light into `helion:glass_surface` (own depth), and the texel color times the vertex color with the texel alpha into `helion:glass_tint`. Aervium injects its distant water into `render`, so the glass pass stays a separate method. Publishes the targets in `WaterResults`.
- Ocean swell: with the wave simulation the stage also binds the shore mask (`ShoreSampler`); `water/surface.vsh` displaces open water vertices by `helionOceanHeight` scaled by `helionOceanOpenness` (`include/helion_water_waves.glsl`), see Dynamic Water.
- Waves: binds the latest wave state (repeat, linear) and adds its slope to the wave normal within 60 blocks, fading from 44; rain rings always come from `helionRainRipples` (the simulation carries no rain drops).
- Targets: The surface pass also writes `helion:water_tint` (RGBA8, the vertex color of water, that is its biome color, alpha 1; zero elsewhere) and, with realistic water (`reflections.realisticWater`), first copies the opaque scene color into `helion:water_refraction` with `copyTextureToTexture`, so the composite can show what lies under the water. Surface uniforms carry the rain level and game seconds for rain ripples on water.
- Depends on: `TerrainSource`, `WaterResources`, `WaterPipelines`, `WaterResults`, `GlossyMask`.

#### ScreenReflectionStage, ReflectionSettings
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/ScreenReflectionStage.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/ReflectionSettings.java`
- Role: Screen space reflections (`reflections.screenSpace`, on in the Experimental preset; the water march also runs whenever realistic water is on, the setting then only decides polished blocks). The stage also runs without that setting while rain wetness is published (then only the surface sheen target is used and nothing is published for the water composite). For every terrain pixel of the geometry buffer the second target gets the surface sheen, composited by `ssr_polished` with premultiplied alpha: the polished reflection of glossy blocks (with the setting, and only when `GeometryBuffer.tracesSpecular()` is false; the path tracer reflects them itself) and the rain wetness of `include/helion_wetness.glsl` (darkening per material, a sky sheen by Fresnel, puddles with rain ripples that march the reflected ray, the ray traced reflection from `rt_reflection` where it misses and one was traced, else the physical sky or the fog color). Two pipelines: with the vanilla fog and with the physical sky color. Pass `ssr` after the water surface: for every flat water pixel and, where the water target is empty, every glass pixel (from `helion:glass_surface` and its depth) marches the reflected view ray in camera relative world space (48 growing steps up to about 190 blocks, 5 refinement steps) against the depth of the lit opaque scene, and writes the scene color at the hit with an edge fade as alpha into `helion:screen_reflection` (RGBA16_FLOAT, full resolution). Opaque glossy pixels (light target alpha 0.75, only with the geometry buffer) without a translucent surface in front march from the scene depth along the geometry normal and write the reflection (Schlick, F0 0.15, strength 0.4, faded over 24 blocks, the first on-screen sky pixel when nothing is hit) with alpha 0 into `helion:polished_reflection`, which pass `ssr_polished` adds over the scene before translucent geometry. Mobs and players are in the opaque scene, so they show in the water when they are on screen. Publishes the target in `WaterResults`.
- Depends on: `WaterResources`, `WaterPipelines`, `WaterResults`, `FullscreenPass`.

#### RainWetnessStage, RainWetness, Wetness, WeatherSource, WeatherState, UnderwaterView
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/RainWetnessStage.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/RainWetness.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/Wetness.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WeatherSource.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WeatherState.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/UnderwaterView.java`
- Role: Rain wetness (`reflections.rainWetness`). `RainWetnessStage`, right after the clear stage, every frame and without a pass, publishes the `UnderwaterView` while the camera is in water (sky light at the camera, water surface height above the eye, floor depth below it, biome water color) and, with the setting, feeds the `WeatherState` of the frame (rain level, game seconds, whether it rains in the camera biome, lightning flash) into `RainWetness` (owned by `WaterResources`), which wets surfaces in 30 s and dries them in 90 s, fills puddles in 90 s and dries them in 240 s, and skips frames after a time jump; the `Wetness` (surfaces, puddles, rain, seconds) is published in `WaterResults` while it is visible. `saturate()` lets the visual test start wet. While wetness is published it also refreshes `WaterRainCover` from the `WaveSource` (16 rows per frame); the water surface pass (rain ripples), the screen reflection pass (wet surfaces, puddles and films) and the water composite (glass drops) bind it as `RainCoverSampler`, so nothing under a roof, also a glass roof, gets rain.
- Depends on: `WaterResources`, `WaterResults`.

#### Footprints, Footprint, FootprintSource
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/Footprints.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/Footprint.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/FootprintSource.java`
- Role: Wet footprints. `RainWetnessStage` asks the `FootprintSource` for the steps of the frame (world position, yaw, foot, game seconds) and keeps the newest 32 in `Footprints` (owned by `WaterResources`) for 45 seconds; the visible ones go to `WaterResults` and into the screen reflection uniform, where `helionFootprints` darkens and wets a small oval per print, fading with its age.
- Depends on: `WaterResources`, `WaterResults`.

#### HeatShimmerStage
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/HeatShimmerStage.java`
- Role: Pass `heat_shimmer` after transparent geometry, with realistic water and the geometry buffer, only while lava may be near (`WeatherState.lavaNearby`) and the camera is not in water: copies the scene into `helion:heat_source` and redraws the pixels on or up to 8 taps (1.2 % of the screen height each) above lava in the geometry light target (`HELION_GEOMETRY_LAVA`) with a rising sine wobble of the copy (`water/heat_shimmer.fsh`), strongest right above the lava.
- Depends on: `WaterResources` (uniform ring), `WaterPipelines.HEAT_SHIMMER`, `GeometryBuffer`.

#### UnderwaterStage
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/UnderwaterStage.java`
- Role: Pass `underwater` (with realistic water, only while the camera is in water): copies the scene into `helion:underwater_scene` and redraws it with `water/underwater.fsh` (no blending): water optics from the camera biome color, column depth and rain, per channel extinction up to the surface, light shafts marched along the view ray from the sun or moon refracted at the surface, a depth color shift of surfaces and caustics on surfaces facing the refracted sun. `RainWetnessStage` also asks the weather source every frame to push the vanilla underwater fog out while realistic water is on. See `docs/architecture/water-optics.md`.
- Depends on: `WaterResources`, `WaterPipelines`, `WaterResults`, `UnderwaterView`, `SkyLight`, `FullscreenPass`.

#### WaterWaveStage, WaterWaves, WaterShoreMask, WaterShoreDistance, WaterRainCover, WaveSource, WaveImpulse
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterWaveStage.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterWaves.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaveSource.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaveImpulse.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterShoreMask.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterShoreDistance.java`, `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterRainCover.java`
- Role: Interactive water surface (with `reflections.realisticWater`). `WaterWaves` (owned by `WaterResources`) keeps two persistent 1024 x 1024 RGBA16_FLOAT targets (height and previous height, 8 cells per block, 128 x 128 blocks around the camera, addressed by world cell modulo 512 so the window scrolls without copies), runs fixed 60 Hz steps from a time backlog (at most 4 per frame, one reset step after a time jump), one uniform ring per step, and queues splashes until a step consumes them. Pass `water_waves` (before the water surface) draws `water/wave_step.fsh` once per step, ping-ponging the two targets, and publishes the latest state in `WaterResults`. `WaveSource` gives the `WaveImpulse`s of the frame (world position, radius, strength, splash or continuous). `WaterShoreMask` (owned by `WaterWaves`) is the R8 block mask of columns that hold waves, 16 of its 128 rows rescanned per frame through `WaveSource.holdsWaves` and uploaded before the steps; land cells (0x80) only move while puddles fill, never mixing with water cells, so waves reflect from shores. `WaterShoreDistance` turns the shore mask into a distance to the nearest land column every frame (two pass 3-4 chamfer transform in window space, so the toroidal seam is never crossed, at most 16 blocks) and uploads it as an R8 texture with the same world mapping, which the surface pass and the water composite sample for the shore waves and their breaking foam. `WaterRainCover` (owned by `WaterResources`) is an R32_FLOAT texture of the rain floor (`WaveSource.rainFloor`) of 64 x 64 columns around the camera, addressed by world column modulo 64, 16 rows rescanned per frame by `WaterReflectionStage`; the glass composite reads it to put rain drops only on glass that rain can reach.
- Depends on: `WaterResources`, `WaterPipelines`, `WaterResults`, `FullscreenPass`.

#### WaterReflectionStage
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterReflectionStage.java`
- Role: When the water surface and traced or screen space reflections exist, publishes a `SceneReflectionPass` (binding the resolved clouds and the cloud sky map of the `SceneCloudPass` when present, and the rain level as turbidity) that `TransparentGeometryStage` draws over the scene after the translucent geometry: the screen space reflection where it hit, else the traced reflection (or the physical sky in the reflected direction with sun and moon, or the vanilla fog color) weighted by Schlick Fresnel, over water darkened by its thickness toward a scattering color lit by the sky. With realistic water it instead replaces every water pixel; surfaces seen from below get Snell's window and total internal reflection, surfaces seen from above: the opaque scene copy, offset by the wave normal by up to 3 % of the screen where the offset pixel is still water, attenuated per channel by the thickness (extinction from clear water 0.30, 0.07, 0.05 per block mixed half and half with the inverse of the linear biome tint), plus the teal-shifted biome tint times the sky above scattered into the lost part, caustics on shallow bottoms by day, mixed with the reflection by Fresnel, plus sun and moon glints. Glass from `helion:glass_surface` is drawn over the water when it is nearer: without realistic water a faint Fresnel reflection, with it the refraction copy behind the glass with a slight wobble, aerial fog, the stain of the glass tint and the reflection (traced or screen space where no water lies behind, else the sky); rain drops only where the rain cover lets rain reach the glass, and with water behind the glass the drops bend the water pixel they show; see Glass in `docs/architecture/water-optics.md`.
- Depends on: `WaterResources`, `WaterPipelines`, `WaterRainCover`, `SceneSkyColor`, `FullscreenPass`.

#### WaterPipelines
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterPipelines.java`
- Role: Water formats, uniform and sampler names, the screen space reflection pipelines (two targets, with the vanilla fog or the physical sky color), the polished composite and the two water composite pipelines (`water/reflection` with the vanilla fog and `water/reflection_physical_sky` with the sky color bindings), blended with premultiplied alpha.
- Depends on: `HelionPipelines`, `PhysicalSkyPipelines`.

#### WaterResources
- Path: `src/main/java/com/aryston/helion/render/water/WaterResources.java`
- Role: Owned by `HelionRenderCore`: the rain wetness state, the uniform rings of the surface pass and of the glass pass (water sprite bounds, wave strength, glass pass flag), the screen pass (view projection and its inverse, flags, wetness, camera position modulo 1024 for the puddle noise) and the composite (inverse view projection, reflection strength), and the compiled composite program with a one-time warning when it is missing.

#### WaterResults, SceneReflectionPass
