# Helion Code Map: Source Files and Asset Folders

Part of the [Helion code map](../CODEMAP.md). Every file under `src/` that is not a Java class, and every asset and data folder.

## Source Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `src/main/templates/META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml` | Mod metadata template filled from `gradle.properties`: dependencies (Arkea required, so the game refuses to start without it), discouraged Iris, mixin config. |
| `src/main/resources/helion.mixins.json` | Mixin configuration listing `ChunkSectionsToRenderAccessor`, `EntityRendererMixin`, `GameRendererMixin`, `LevelRendererAccessor`, `LevelRendererMixin`, `RenderSectionMixin`, `SkyRendererMixin`, `CloudRendererMixin`, `LevelExtractorMixin`, `SectionCompilerMixin`, `WaterFogEnvironmentMixin`, `WaterDropParticleMixin`, and for ray tracing `VulkanFeatureSetsMixin`, `VulkanBackendMixin`, `VulkanConstMixin`, `FrontendGpuDeviceAccessor`, `VulkanDeviceAccessor` and `VulkanCommandEncoderAccessor`. |

## Asset Folders

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/lang/` | `en_us.json` and `tr_tr.json`: config, settings screen (`helion.config.*`), key, toast and parity check texts. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/minecraft/textures/` | Vanilla texture overrides for the realistic rain look (always active, like a resource pack): `environment/rain.png` (256 x 1024, sparse soft pale streaks at 9 to 18 % alpha instead of the blue dashes) and `particle/splash_0` to `splash_3` (16 x 16, one to three soft droplets, blended because `WaterDropParticleMixin` draws rain drops and splashes translucent). Generated by a small Java script, not drawn. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/textures/gui/` | `logo.png` (48 x 48, linear filtered through `logo.png.mcmeta`) for the settings sidebar, and `preview/`: placeholder before and after images of bloom and the physical sky from the design handoff, to be replaced with real captures. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/image/` | Bloom and image fragment shaders: `bloom_prefilter`, `bloom_downsample`, `bloom_upsample`, `composite` (with `HELION_COLOR_GRADING` also the grading table lookup), `bloom_debug`, `sharpen`, `exposure_meter`, `exposure_average`. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/entity/` | Geometry buffer entity shaders `geometry.vsh` and `geometry.fsh`: the vanilla `core/entity` math (also for the item vertex layout with `HELION_ITEM_LAYOUT`) for the scene color plus normal, light and albedo targets, for `EntityGeometryPipelines`. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/terrain/` | Geometry buffer terrain shaders `geometry.vsh` and `geometry.fsh`: vanilla terrain color plus normal (crossed plant planes point up), light (with chunk fade-in) and albedo (with the fog amount in alpha) targets. `translucent.fsh`: the vanilla translucent terrain shader that also discards plain glass cells of the glossy mask. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/rtx/` | Ray tracing: `reflection.csh`, the traced water, glass and puddle reflections that read the hit surface through buffer references from the section surface table, `path_trace.csh`, the path traced light that writes demodulated linear HDR irradiance for every geometry buffer pixel (sun, moon and block light with shadow rays, plus multi-bounce indirect light), `denoise_accumulate.csh` (reprojected temporal accumulation with luminance moments and variance) and `specular_accumulate.csh` (reprojected, neighborhood clamped specular history), `denoise_filter.csh` (variance guided à-trous filter with normal and distance edge stops). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/pathtracing/` | `radiance.fsh`: multiplies the denoised irradiance by the unshaded linear albedo and encodes it into the scene with the Neutral curve, chunk fade and fog. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/light/` | `colors.json`: linear RGB light color per block id, read by `LightColors` as the emission color of the path traced block lights; resource packs can replace it. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/geometry/` | `debug.fsh`: geometry buffer debug views. `terrain_depth.fsh`: copies the depth after the terrain pass into a float target. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/temporal/` | Temporal anti-aliasing fragment shaders: `resolve` (closest-depth reprojection, Catmull-Rom history sample, YCoCg variance clipping, luminance weighted blend, moving object depth in the history alpha with disocclusion rejection and a reactive weight) and `apply` (history back into the scene), `upscale_apply` (history plus the nearest internal depth into the output sized target when upscaling). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/motion_blur/` | Motion blur fragment shaders: `tile_max` (longest camera velocity per tile), `neighbor_max` (longest of the 3 x 3 tiles), `gather` (depth and speed weighted blur along the tile velocity, after McGuire et al. 2012) and `apply` (blurred image back into the scene). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/depth_of_field/` | Depth of field fragment shaders: `focus` (smoothed center focus distance), `downsample` (half resolution color and signed circle of confusion), `gather` (golden-angle gather with foreground and background rules), `blend` (blurred image faded in over the scene by blur size). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/edge_smoothing/` | Edge smoothing fragment shaders: `smooth` (luma edge detection, edge direction, search for the edge ends, sub-pixel blend) and `apply` (result back into the scene). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/clouds/` | Volumetric cloud fragment shaders: `noise` (the tiling noise atlas, written once), `march` (raymarch of the cumulus layer and the cirrus layer into color and distance; with `HELION_CLOUD_SKY_MAP` into the 192 x 192 sky map for water reflections), `resolve` (temporal blend with the reprojected history) and `composite` (blend over the scene). Shared code in `include/helion_clouds.glsl` (the `HelionClouds` uniform block and view rays) and `include/helion_cloud_noise.glsl` (atlas lookup). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/light_shafts/` | Light shaft fragment shaders: `mask` (bright sky around the sun or moon from scene depth, times the cloud transmittance with `HELION_CLOUD_SHAFTS`), `blur` (radial blur toward the light) and `composite` (screen blend of the sun or moon colored shafts over the scene). Shared code in `include/helion_light_shafts.glsl` (the `HelionLightShafts` uniform block and view rays). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/atmosphere/` | Physical sky fragment shaders: `transmittance` and `multiple_scattering` (constant spectral lookup tables, four targets each), `sky_view` (spectral single and multiple scattering per view direction, converted to RGB, for the sun or the moon with `HELION_MOON_VIEW`; Mie single scattering goes to a second target without its phase), `sky` (sky color per pixel), `fog` (distance fog in the physical horizon color over everything with depth, with the vanilla fog distances), `horizon` (average horizon color over 32 directions into a 1 x 1 target) and `sky_light` (sun light, moon light and sky dome irradiance into a 3 x 1 target). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/water/` | Water: `wave_step.fsh` runs one step of the interactive wave equation (velocity damping, entity impulses, rain drops from an integer hash, zero outside the scrolling window and near its edge); `surface.vsh` and `surface.fsh` write the octahedral normal (with small animated waves on flat surfaces), the water thickness to the opaque surface behind and the sky light of the nearest water surface (face normal and the glossy marker for other translucent blocks); `polished_composite.fsh` adds the polished reflection over the scene; `screen_reflection.fsh` marches the reflected ray of each water or glossy pixel, and of glossy opaque pixels into a second target, against the opaque scene depth and returns the scene color at the hit; `reflection.fsh` mixes the traced reflection or the sky (with on-screen clouds or the cloud sky map) by a roughness aware Fresnel, adds GGX sun and moon glints and draws realistic water from the shared water optics and realistic glass (in rain a misted blur of the view behind, two layers of lens drops that slightly magnify the view and straight run trails that clear the mist); `underwater.fsh` redraws a copy of the scene under water with per channel extinction, marched light shafts, depth color shift and caustics. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/include/` | Shared GLSL: `helion_water` (octahedral normals, the water thickness range and the roughness and falling marks in the tint alpha), `helion_water_optics` (absorption, scattering and albedo of water from the biome tint, the column depth and the turbidity), `helion_noise_slope` (analytic gradient noise value and slope over the PCG hash), `helion_cloud_sky_map` (octahedral upper hemisphere mapping of the cloud sky map), `helion_rt_surface` (hit surface lookup with the octahedral face normal and cutout alpha testing for ray queries; the including shader declares `Sections` and `AtlasSampler` first), `helion_water_waves` (six octaves of rotated, drifting gradient noise for the water swell and its slope, four octaves of fast capillary slopes and the slope variance they lose to the pixel footprint, the shore waves and breaking foam from the shore distance texture, and the ocean swell that moves the surface), `helion_caustics` (caustic light from the area change of light refracted through a gradient noise surface, three rotated octaves, and the gain that mostly brightens: darker gaps keep 75 % of the light), `helion_lava` (lava crust: drifting Voronoi cells that cool into dark crust with glowing cracks, modulated by slow swells), `helion_hash` (integer PCG hash of a cell, for noise without the banding of `sin` hashes at large arguments), `helion_rain_cover` (whether rain reaches the cell in front of a surface, from `RainCoverSampler` within 28 blocks; farther always), `helion_wetness` (rain wetness of a geometry buffer pixel: exposure from the sky light, material darkening and sheen, the puddle mask from three octaves of value noise that repeat every 1024 blocks, rain drop ripple normals; included by `water/screen_reflection.fsh`), `helion_rt_entity` (entity hit lookup for ray traced reflections: indices, color, texture coordinate and light read through buffer references, sampled from `EntityTextures[nonuniformEXT(slot)]`, cutout alpha 0.1; the including shader declares `Entities` and `EntityTextures` first), `helion_terrain_vertex` (terrain vertex inputs for `geometry.vsh`: the vanilla block format, or with `AERVIUM_COMPACT_VERTICES` Aervium's 16-byte format decoded to the same position, texture coordinate and light values), `helion_light_level` (the vanilla light level brightness curve and the balanced light color, kept for the coming path traced light, not included yet), `helion_path_tracing` (the vanilla directional shade and the unshaded linear albedo, the irradiance signal values in alpha, emissive geometry detection; shared by `path_trace.csh`, the denoiser and `radiance.fsh`), `helion_rt_light` (the block light records of the path traced light grid: cell lookup, record decoding, the unshadowed falloff and facing of a light, the vanilla lightmap texels and the path traced sky with its ambient floor and `vanillaShare`; the including shader declares `Lights`, `LightmapSampler`, `SkyLightSampler`, `Sun` and `Moon` first; shared by `path_trace.csh` and `reflection.csh`), `helion_geometry` (geometry buffer constants, the block material values of the light target alpha, normal encoding and crossed plant plane detection), `helion_view` (depth and view position), `helion_atmosphere` (Earth atmosphere constants, phase functions, ray-sphere intersection, ground shadow, lookup table sizes and coordinate mappings), `helion_spectrum` (`HelionSpectrum` block, densities, spectral scattering and extinction, spectrum to RGB), `helion_transmittance_lookup` and `helion_scattering_lookup` (samplers and per group lookup of the spectral tables), `helion_sky` (sky settings block, with the moon phase angle and the last rain wetness for the rainbow), `helion_night` (the night sky of the sky pass: star frame from the star angle, two cube map star layers with colors, twinkling and visibility against the sky behind them, Milky Way, planets, meteors, aurora curtains, and the navy night glow that `helion_sky_color` adds to every sky direction), `helion_celestial` (the limb darkened sun disc with its aureole, white once it is bright, and the procedural moon sphere: phase lighting, maria, craters with relief, earthshine), `helion_twilight` (analytic air mass and celestial transmittance, the golden hour weight and the twilight glow toward the sun, the rose afterglow and the pink belt opposite it), `helion_sky_color` (sun and moon sky view lookup with the Mie phase applied per pixel, twilight glow, rain, the sky grade with a golden hour saturation and the blue shift of dim night skies, Neutral encoding, the sun and moon for the sky pass only, the camera relative position of a pixel from its depth and the view ray of a pixel from two points on it, so view bobbing never moves the sky against the world), `helion_color` (sRGB conversion, saturation, the Neutral highlight curve and its inverse, film grade, dither noise and `helionDitherEncoded`, the one step dither of every pass that writes the RGBA8 scene), `helion_exposure` (size of the exposure meter), `helion_color_grading` (trilinear lookup in the 32 x 32 x 32 grading table), `helion_temporal` (`HelionTemporal` block shared by the resolve and upscale passes), `helion_depth_of_field` (`HelionDepthOfField` block, view depth and circle of confusion), `helion_motion_blur` (`HelionMotionBlur` block, camera velocity of a pixel from its depth, tile size), `helion_image` (image settings block including the sharpening strength, scene expansion, tone mapper switch, Filmic highlight bleach, bloom threshold, dither), `helion_bloom_downsample` (13-tap downsample with optional Karis average over a `helionBloomTap` function the including shader defines). |
