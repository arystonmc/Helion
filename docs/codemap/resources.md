# Helion Code Map: Source Files and Asset Folders

Part of the [Helion code map](../CODEMAP.md). Every file under `src/` that is not a Java class, and every asset and data folder.

## Source Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `src/main/templates/META-INF/neoforge.mods.toml` | Mod metadata template filled from `gradle.properties`: dependencies (Arkea required, so the game refuses to start without it), discouraged Iris, mixin config. |
| `src/main/resources/helion.mixins.json` | Mixin configuration listing `ChunkSectionsToRenderAccessor`, `EntityRendererMixin`, `GameRendererMixin`, `LevelRendererAccessor`, `LevelRendererMixin`, `RenderSectionMixin`, `SkyRendererMixin`, `CloudRendererMixin`, `LevelExtractorMixin`, `SectionCompilerMixin`, and for ray tracing `VulkanFeatureSetsMixin`, `VulkanBackendMixin`, `VulkanConstMixin`, `FrontendGpuDeviceAccessor`, `VulkanDeviceAccessor` and `VulkanCommandEncoderAccessor`. |

## Asset Folders

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/lang/` | `en_us.json` and `tr_tr.json`: config, settings screen (`helion.config.*`), key, toast and parity check texts. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/textures/gui/` | `logo.png` (48 x 48, linear filtered through `logo.png.mcmeta`) for the settings sidebar, and `preview/`: placeholder before and after images of ambient occlusion, bloom, shadows and the physical sky from the design handoff, to be replaced with real captures. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/ambient_occlusion/` | Ambient occlusion fragment shaders: `view_depth`, `gtao`, `denoise`, `denoise_resolve`, `apply` (with `HELION_INDIRECT_SHARE` also scaled by the indirect share). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/image/` | Bloom and image fragment shaders: `bloom_prefilter`, `bloom_downsample`, `bloom_upsample`, `composite` (with `HELION_COLOR_GRADING` also the grading table lookup), `bloom_debug`, `sharpen`, `exposure_meter`, `exposure_average`. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/terrain/` | Geometry buffer terrain shaders `geometry.vsh` and `geometry.fsh`: vanilla terrain color plus normal (crossed plant planes point up), light (with chunk fade-in, raised by dynamic lights with `HELION_DYNAMIC_LIGHTS`) and albedo (with the fog amount in alpha) targets. `dynamic_light.vsh`: the vanilla terrain vertex shader with the block light of every vertex raised to the dynamic light level, used with the vanilla terrain fragment shader. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/voxel/` | `slice.fsh`: the voxel slice view, a top-down map of the voxel volume at the camera's height. `direct_light.fsh`: the traced colored block light of a terrain pixel, with the dynamic lights and entity boxes of the `HelionVoxelDynamic` block (light grid lookup, grid walk and light exit from `include/helion_voxel_trace.glsl`). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/rtx/` | RTX spike and ray tracing: `sun_shadow.csh`, the compute shader of the ray tracing validation pass that traces each half resolution pixel toward the sun or moon against the section scene; `sun_shadow_view.fsh`, its blocked pixels drawn dark blue over the output; `shadow_mask.csh`, the raw Vulkan compute shader that traces the brightest light of each half resolution pixel with `rayQueryEXT` against the box scene and with the voxel grid walk, writes both results into the mask and counts their agreement; `mask_view.fsh`, the mask drawn over the output. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/voxel_light/` | `colors.json`: linear RGB light color per block id for voxel lighting; resource packs can replace it. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/geometry/` | `debug.fsh`: geometry buffer debug views. `terrain_depth.fsh`: copies the depth after the terrain pass into a float target. |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/lighting/` | Deferred lighting fragment shader `deferred_shading` (light from the geometry buffer light levels, see `helion_deferred_light`, then albedo times light, HDR above the vanilla cap, fog and chunk fade-in; light-only view with `HELION_LIGHT_ONLY`; with `HELION_SHADOWS` the color is darkened by the shadow transmission after the vanilla brightness curve). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/temporal/` | Temporal anti-aliasing fragment shaders: `resolve` (closest-depth reprojection, Catmull-Rom history sample, YCoCg variance clipping, luminance weighted blend, moving object depth in the history alpha with disocclusion rejection and a reactive weight) and `apply` (history back into the scene), `upscale_apply` (history plus the nearest internal depth into the output sized target when upscaling). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/motion_blur/` | Motion blur fragment shaders: `tile_max` (longest camera velocity per tile), `neighbor_max` (longest of the 3 x 3 tiles), `gather` (depth and speed weighted blur along the tile velocity, after McGuire et al. 2012) and `apply` (blurred image back into the scene). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/depth_of_field/` | Depth of field fragment shaders: `focus` (smoothed center focus distance), `downsample` (half resolution color and signed circle of confusion), `gather` (golden-angle gather with foreground and background rules), `blend` (blurred image faded in over the scene by blur size). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/edge_smoothing/` | Edge smoothing fragment shaders: `smooth` (luma edge detection, edge direction, search for the edge ends, sub-pixel blend) and `apply` (result back into the scene). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/clouds/` | Volumetric cloud fragment shaders: `noise` (the tiling noise atlas, written once), `march` (raymarch of the cumulus layer and the cirrus layer into color and distance), `resolve` (temporal blend with the reprojected history) and `composite` (blend over the scene). Shared code in `include/helion_clouds.glsl` (the `HelionClouds` uniform block and view rays) and `include/helion_cloud_noise.glsl` (atlas lookup). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/light_shafts/` | Light shaft fragment shaders: `mask` (bright sky around the sun or moon from scene depth, times the cloud transmittance with `HELION_CLOUD_SHAFTS`), `blur` (radial blur toward the light) and `composite` (screen blend of the sun or moon colored shafts over the scene). Shared code in `include/helion_light_shafts.glsl` (the `HelionLightShafts` uniform block and view rays). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/atmosphere/` | Physical sky fragment shaders: `transmittance` and `multiple_scattering` (constant spectral lookup tables, four targets each), `sky_view` (spectral single and multiple scattering per view direction, converted to RGB, for the sun or the moon with `HELION_MOON_VIEW`; Mie single scattering goes to a second target without its phase), `sky` (sky color per pixel), `fog` (distance fog in the physical horizon color over everything with depth, with the vanilla fog distances), `horizon` (average horizon color over 32 directions into a 1 x 1 target) and `sky_light` (sun light, moon light and sky dome irradiance into a 3 x 1 target). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/shadow/` | Shadow shaders: `caster.vsh` and `caster.fsh` (terrain positions only, alpha cutout for cutout layers, crossed plant planes skipped, depth only) and `mask.fsh` (cascade selection, normal and light offset, distance fade, R8 visibility mask). |
| `src/main/resources/assets/helion/shaders/include/` | Shared GLSL: `helion_terrain_vertex` (terrain vertex inputs for `geometry.vsh` and `caster.vsh`: the vanilla block format, or with `AERVIUM_COMPACT_VERTICES` Aervium's 16-byte format decoded to the same position, texture coordinate and light values), `helion_shadow` (`HelionShadow` block, one shadow map sampler per cascade and one entity shadow sampler per near cascade, 4 x 4 tent filtered lookups, the entity lookup turning reversed depth back into the cascade depth), `helion_voxel` (voxel volume addressing like `VoxelVolumeLayout`: block to ring slot, brick and slice texel, ready-slot and coarse lookups, occupancy and emission of a voxel byte), `helion_voxel_trace` (shared by the voxel light pass and the RTX spike: light grid lookup, light-blocking test, the 3D grid walk with the coarse level, the open face of a light block, the volume edge distance and the volume position of a pixel; the including shader declares the voxel samplers, `VolumeMin`, `InverseViewProjection` and `CameraToVolume`), `helion_light_level` (the vanilla light level brightness curve, shared by `helion_lighting` and the voxel light pass), `helion_geometry` (geometry buffer constants, normal encoding and crossed plant plane detection), `helion_view` (depth and view position), `helion_atmosphere` (Earth atmosphere constants, phase functions, ray-sphere intersection, ground shadow, lookup table sizes and coordinate mappings), `helion_spectrum` (`HelionSpectrum` block, densities, spectral scattering and extinction, spectrum to RGB), `helion_transmittance_lookup` and `helion_scattering_lookup` (samplers and per group lookup of the spectral tables), `helion_sky` (sky settings block, with the moon phase angle), `helion_night` (the night sky of the sky pass: star frame from the star angle, two cube map star layers with colors, twinkling and visibility against the sky behind them, Milky Way, planets, meteors, aurora curtains, and the navy night glow that `helion_sky_color` adds to every sky direction), `helion_celestial` (the limb darkened sun disc with its aureole, white once it is bright, and the procedural moon sphere: phase lighting, maria, craters with relief, earthshine), `helion_twilight` (analytic air mass and celestial transmittance, the golden hour weight and the twilight glow toward the sun, the rose afterglow and the pink belt opposite it), `helion_sky_color` (sun and moon sky view lookup with the Mie phase applied per pixel, twilight glow, rain, the sky grade with a golden hour saturation and the blue shift of dim night skies, Neutral encoding, the sun and moon for the sky pass only, the camera relative position of a pixel from its depth and the view ray of a pixel from two points on it, so view bobbing never moves the sky against the world), `helion_dynamic_light` (`HelionDynamicLights` block and the block light of a vertex raised to the level of the nearest dynamic light minus its distance; without `HELION_DYNAMIC_LIGHTS` it returns the light unchanged), `helion_deferred_light` (light of a terrain pixel in vanilla units without the cap and its daylight reference; with `HELION_PHYSICAL_SKY_LIGHT` the sky light color follows the physical sky light per pixel normal at the vanilla brightness), `helion_lighting` (lighting settings block, the vanilla light map terms with bilinear level interpolation, ambient term, boss and darkness adjustment and the brightness option), `helion_sky_light` (physical sky light parts per pixel normal: sun, moon, a desaturated sky dome toward the sky and ground bounce light toward the ground, and the shadow transmission: lit sun, moon and dome light against the shadowed sum, weighted by the share of sky light in the pixel), `helion_ambient_occlusion` (settings block, edge packing), `helion_ambient_occlusion_denoise` (edge aware blur), `helion_color` (sRGB conversion, saturation, the Neutral highlight curve and its inverse, film grade, dither noise), `helion_exposure` (size of the exposure meter), `helion_color_grading` (trilinear lookup in the 32 x 32 x 32 grading table), `helion_temporal` (`HelionTemporal` block shared by the resolve and upscale passes), `helion_depth_of_field` (`HelionDepthOfField` block, view depth and circle of confusion), `helion_motion_blur` (`HelionMotionBlur` block, camera velocity of a pixel from its depth, tile size), `helion_image` (image settings block including the sharpening strength, scene expansion, tone mapper switch, Filmic highlight bleach, bloom threshold, dither), `helion_bloom_downsample` (13-tap downsample with optional Karis average over a `helionBloomTap` function the including shader defines). |
